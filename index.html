About Me

Hi, I'm Angelina.

I'm a makeup artist based in Miami.

I've loved beauty since I was young, and today I get to turn that passion into something meaningful: helping women feel confident, soft, and beautiful in their own skin.

Known for "photoshop effect" in real life 💄

I specialize in glowing skin, soft glam, and enhancing your natural beauty rather than covering it. For me, makeup is not just about appearance — it's about how you feel when you look in the mirror ✨

Questions

What is your cancelation policy? +
You can cancel your booking. But deposits are not refundable or transferable.
Do you provide services for any events? +
Yes, I provide services for any special occasion, event, or wedding.
Should my hair be wet or dry? +
Hair should be clean and dry for the styling.
Are lashes included in the price? +
Yes. Fake lashes for bride and bridal party will be provided upon request with no cost. You can also use your own lashes if desired.
What additional fees should I know about? +
1. Clients are responsible for any parking fees.

2. Depending on the location and getting-ready time, travel fees and early morning fees may apply.

3. Add-ons such as hair extension placement, styling, and blowouts will be charged additionally.
Which forms of payment are accepted? +
Payments are accepted through Zelle.
Do you work on holidays? +
Yes, the double rate may apply. Please inquire for more information.
